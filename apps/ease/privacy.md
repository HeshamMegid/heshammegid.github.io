Ease is a migraine tracker made by Hesham Abd-Elmegid, an independent developer. This policy explains what Ease stores, where it goes and how to delete it.

## What Ease stores in your account

Ease creates an account for you automatically, without asking for your name or email. Everything below is stored in that account on Google Firebase, so you can only see it when you're signed in to that account.

- **Your attacks:** when each started, severity, duration, symptoms, triggers, relief methods (with doses and how well they worked) and any notes you write.
- **Your settings:** your symptom, trigger and relief method lists, logging steps, preventive medications and their doses, and whether reminders are on.
- **Sign-in details, if you add them:** your email address if you sign in with email and password (Firebase handles the password, and we never see it), or the identifier and email address Apple shares if you use Sign in with Apple (which can be a private relay address).
- **Premium:** whether you have Premium, and when you first and last upgraded. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details.
- **Weather at each attack, if you allow location:** barometric pressure, temperature and humidity when the attack started, how much the pressure had changed, and the location the weather was checked for.
- **Sleep before each attack, if you allow access to sleep data in Apple Health:** how long you slept the night before, and how that compares with your usual sleep.
- **Pressure alerts, if you turn them on:** your approximate location (rounded to about 11 km), your time zone, alert settings and a push notification token for each of your devices.
- **How you found Ease:** if you installed Ease from an Apple Ads ad, Apple's attribution data, made up of the campaign, ad group, keyword and App Store country. This doesn't identify you.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Ease reads headaches logged by other apps and your sleep data from Apple Health, and writes the attacks you log in Ease to Apple Health if you turn that on. Headaches read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone and aren't copied to your Ease account. The only Health information stored in your account is the sleep summary for each attack described above. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

## Location and weather

Ease asks for your location only while you're using it, to check the local barometric pressure. Your location is sent to Apple Weather to get the forecast and pressure history, and Ease never tracks your location in the background.

## Pressure alerts

If you turn on pressure alerts, Ease stores your approximate location, rounded to about 11 km, and your time zone, so our server can check the pressure forecast for your area each evening and send you a notification before a significant change. The server asks Apple Weather for the forecast and sends alerts through Apple's push notification service using Firebase Cloud Messaging. The location updates only when you move to a different area, and Ease deletes it when you turn pressure alerts off.

## Insights summary

The written summary on the Insights screen is created on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. Your data isn't sent anywhere to create it.

## Reminders

Follow-up and streak reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They can appear on your Lock Screen.

## Reports and sharing

PDF reports and year in review images are created on your iPhone. They go only where you send them from the share sheet, and from then on that app's privacy policy applies.

## Analytics

Ease uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how a few features are used, such as how often the Premium screen is opened and from where, completed purchases, the year in review, and turning pressure alerts on or off. Along with these events, Firebase automatically collects basic information such as device model, iOS version, app version, country and an app instance ID. No migraine data, notes or health data are sent to analytics. Ease doesn't use your device's advertising identifier and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

## Who processes your data

Ease relies on these services to work:

- **Google Firebase**, for your account, storing your data, server functions, push notifications, analytics and app configuration.
- **Apple**, for Apple Health, Apple Weather, Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, push notifications, App Store purchases and Apple Ads attribution.

They process data on our behalf to provide these features. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers or anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Firebase. Access rules let each account read and write only its own data.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Attacks:** delete an attack in the app at any time, and it's removed from your account straight away.
- **Pressure alert location:** deleted as soon as you turn pressure alerts off.
- **Your account:** email [ease@hesh.am](mailto:ease@hesh.am) to delete your account and everything in it. We'll do it within 30 days. Deleting the app from your iPhone doesn't delete your account.

## Children

Ease isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
