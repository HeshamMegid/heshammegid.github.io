Hi, I'm Hesham 👋

Independent app developer

Each of these started as an app I needed and couldn't find, and I design and build every one of them myself.

TracKit began in 2013 as a simple tracker for my own numbers, and I've spent more than a decade shaping it into what it is today.

Ease came from living with migraines and wanting a better way to log attacks and figure out what triggers them.

Brisk started as a feature I wanted in TracKit, long-term workout goals that fill in from Apple Health, and grew into an app of its own.
